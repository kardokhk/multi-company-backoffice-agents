Ground rules

  • The assistant never sends, accepts or pays anything. Replies wait in the Drafts folder; meetings go in as tentative holds.
  • Clashes are checked across all four calendars at once, in code, not by the model.
  • Bank details, phone numbers and email addresses are masked before the model sees any text.
  • Fraud checks run before the model and do not depend on it: lookalike domains, impersonated colleagues, fake ATO links, bank-detail changes, hidden instructions.
  • Your tone comes from examples of your own sent mail, picked fresh for every draft. No model is trained on your email.
